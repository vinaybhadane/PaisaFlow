# 01 — Product Vision and Principles

## Product definition
PaisaFlow is an AI-powered personal finance coach built around a **Living Money Model**: a structured, time-aware representation of a user's income, expenses, recurring commitments, budgets, savings and goals. It updates when new transactions or explicit corrections arrive. Deterministic services calculate; AI explains.

## Vision and mission
**Vision:** Make personal finances understandable and actionable through a financial model that evolves with a person's life.  
**Mission:** Reduce effort and uncertainty in understanding transaction history, identifying recurring obligations, maintaining realistic budgets, planning goals and evaluating changes before they happen.

## Target personas
### Early-career professional
Monthly salary, multiple payment methods, rent, utilities, food, transport and subscriptions. Wants a savings goal and less manual categorization.

### Student / first-job earner
Limited or irregular income, needs plain-language explanations and achievable goals.

### Freelancer
Variable income and irregular payment dates. Needs cash-flow visibility and conservative planning.

The demo must include at least one salary persona and one irregular-income persona, both fictional.

## Value proposition
"Understand your money today, see how your choices affect tomorrow, and get guidance that adapts as your financial life changes."

## Differentiation
An ordinary tracker lists transactions and charts. PaisaFlow learns from corrections, identifies recurring commitments, preserves financial history, connects goals to cash flow, simulates choices and explains nudges with evidence.

## Product principles
1. Accuracy before cleverness.
2. Explainability for every important inference.
3. User control: correct, dismiss, undo and delete.
4. Adaptation only from explicit corrections and supported evidence.
5. Honest uncertainty; forecasts are not promises.
6. Privacy by design and data minimization.
7. Supportive, non-shaming nudges.
8. No securities recommendations.
9. Accessible design.
10. Synthetic data only during the hackathon.

## In scope
Synthetic PDF/CSV/SMS ingestion; transaction normalization and categorization; merchant aliases; correction learning; recurring detection; budget and cash-flow analysis; persistent profile; goals; what-if simulation; AI explanation; nudges; monthly reports.

## Out of scope
Real bank connection, reading real SMS/email, payments/transfers, subscription cancellation, credit scoring, loan underwriting, tax filing, investment recommendations or guarantees.

## Success measures
Measure extraction accuracy, categorization precision/recall, recurring precision/recall, false positives, arithmetic reconciliation, correction-rule success, AI groundedness, user task completion, responsiveness and usability. Do not claim unmeasured performance or generalize synthetic results to real-world users.

## Brand
Name: PaisaFlow. Tagline: Your Money. Your Flow. Your Future. Voice: clear, calm, supportive, practical, non-judgmental and transparent. Avoid fear, shame and exaggerated savings claims.
